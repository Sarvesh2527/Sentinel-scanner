Responsible testing

Ethical by design.

Ethical use only

Active exploitation checks (XSS and SQL injection) are designed for intentionally vulnerable practice targets — DVWA and OWASP Juice Shop running locally in Docker. Only scan systems you own or are explicitly authorised to test.

Sentinel is a final-year academic project. Active penetration-testing checks are intended for intentionally vulnerable practice applications running in a local lab. Do not scan systems you do not own or lack explicit authorisation to test.
